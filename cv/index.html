Projects

  • 2026
    MorphMimic: World Model and Policy Sharing Across Humanoids
    • Shared one morphology-conditioned world model and motion-tracking policy across Unitree G1, AgiBot X2 Ultra, and Booster T2, completing over 99.7% of 128,786 clips on each robot in simulation.

    Safe Learning with Control Barrier Functions
    • Integrated control barrier functions into reinforcement learning training through in-training safety filtering and closed-form discrete-time constraints.
    • Validated obstacle avoidance and stair climbing on Unitree G1 without a runtime safety filter; extended the framework to perception-aware humanoid dodgeball.
    • Built a training-free contextual safety filter for text-conditioned humanoid motion generators.

    • Developed an agentic planner and a teacher-student framework that distills complementary whole-body controllers for zero-shot loco-manipulation without task-specific demonstrations.
    • Deployed on Unitree G1 for table-top sorting, object transport, and bimanual handling.
